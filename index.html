ABOUT THE CLUB

A new way to compete at Cleveland.

Cleveland High School Pickleball is a developing school program for students who want to learn the game, improve their skills, make friends, and compete.

Our goal is to build a sustainable school pickleball program that can grow from practices and club play into organized competition with other high schools.

01

Learn

Build the fundamentals, understand strategy, and become a smarter player.

02

Play

Get on the court, play with other students, and improve through competition.

03

Compete

Help establish a Cleveland program that can compete with schools across the area.

QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Who can join?

All students of Cleveland High School and local homeschool students.

Do I need my own paddle?

Yes, bring a paddle, water, and appropriate court shoes.

Is this a varsity sport?

This program is currently only a club, but help us make it a varsity sport!

How much does it cost?

It is free to join the club, however you do have the option to buy apparel.
